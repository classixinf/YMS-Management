04 / 10
Operational journey

One visit. One timeline. Every important event captured.

Appointment — supplier/customer, purpose, material, expected time, appointed person.
Gate In / ANPR — plate captured, appointment matched, driver/visit verified when needed.
Weigh In — gross/tare measurement linked to the truck visit.
Waiting / Yard — truck status and waiting time become measurable.
Loading / Unloading — operation starts and ends are timestamped.
Weigh Out — final quantity and variance are calculated.
Gate Out / ANPR — exit time closes the visit automatically.
Notification — the appointed person receives the completion/exception message.

06 / 10
Exception management

Automation should accelerate normal operations — not hide exceptions.

Normal

Appointment + ANPR match

Truck plate matches an active appointment. The system creates/updates the truck visit and moves it into the expected workflow.

Exception

Truck mismatch

The transporter changed the vehicle, plate is unreadable, appointment is missing, or multiple possible appointments exist. Operator confirms before proceeding.

Unknown truck

ANPR sees a vehicle with no active appointment → alert security → create controlled exception visit if authorized.

Low ANPR confidence

Capture plate image + vehicle image + confidence score → ask operator to verify rather than trusting a weak read.

Operational delay

Waiting time crosses threshold → notify responsible team and expose bottleneck on the dashboard.

Recommended roadmap

Start with control. Add sophistication only where it creates value.

Optimization

Management decision: approve requirements discovery and a controlled pilot at one gate / one warehouse flow before scaling across the operation.
